Welcome to My Website!

Daewon Kim is a Ph.D. student in Economics at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa. He received his B.A. in Economics from Kyung Hee University in Seoul, Republic of Korea.

His primary research fields are Game Theory and Information Economics, with applications to Law and Economics and Industrial Organization. His current research studies equilibrium refinements and dynamic games, including stochastic games, together with strategic information problems. Using mathematical and geometric methods, he examines how equilibrium behavior and economic predictions depend on informational environments, institutional structures, and dynamic interactions.

More broadly, his research seeks to extend insights from game theory and information economics to a wide range of economic problems. While his work is primarily theoretical, he is also interested in empirical and experimental approaches when they can complement or deepen theoretical analysis.
